# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project community. Be
constructive, assume good intent, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and
respect different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
publication of private information, and sustained disruption. Maintainers may
remove content or participation that violates these expectations. Report conduct
concerns privately through the maintainer contact method on their GitHub profile.
